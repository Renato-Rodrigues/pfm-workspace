# Glossary

Project-specific words, and only those. If a term can be recovered by reading the function
that implements it, it does not belong here. Mathematics is in `docs/MODEL.md`; decisions are
in `docs/adr/`.

---

## The model

### PFM — Political Feasibility Model
The whole system: the estimated frontier, the packages that build and fit it (`mrpfm`, `pfm`),
the REMIND module that consumes it, and the coupling between them. **This is the only name
used in current documents and in the paper.** Code and artifacts used the older `psm*`
prefix until 2026-10-02 (`runPSMSweep()`, `selected-models-psm.yml`, `psm-sweep`); they denote
the same object. Run-Groups written before the rename (`v5` and earlier) keep the old file
names, and the code still reads them (`design-notes/0005` D21).

### Feasibility frontier
The conditional *maximum* policy stringency associated with a country's drivers, estimated as
a stochastic production frontier. Countries sit on or below it. It is a **statistical**
frontier — the best observed among comparable countries — not a physical or legal limit.

### Political slack
The one-sided residual $u \ge 0$: the distance between what a country's drivers permit and what
it actually did. The symmetric half of the residual is ordinary noise.

### Efficiency ratio ($E$) — the implementability factor
$E = S/S^{*}$, the share of its own political potential a country has realised. The principled
implementability multiplier, because it compares a polity to **its own** possibility. The naive
$S/10$ compares it to a universal maximum and is rejected.

### Relative gap
$1 - E$. Always the frontier-relative gap, bounded in $[0,1]$. Distinct from the **absolute
gap** $S^{*}-S$ (index points, dominated by ceiling size) and from the mild-progression gap
$(S^{*}-S)/S$ (unbounded). Conflating them is a documented error — `docs/PITFALLS.md` §7.

### Sector: Bulk / Diffuse
The two political regimes the model is estimated on. **Bulk** = electricity + industry: few,
large, organised actors. **Diffuse** = buildings + transport: costs land on households
directly. The four-sector split (electricity, industry, buildings, transport) is used only for
adjustment speeds.

### Actor power — Innovator and Incumbent
Two shares computed from the IAM's own energy system: how far the clean transition has gone,
and how much fossil capital is still in place. **This is the channel through which a REMIND
pathway talks back to the model.** They must enter **split**, never as a composite difference —
the composite makes projections scenario-blind under the extrapolation guard.

### Saturating transform
Actor power enters as $\tilde A = A/(A+\bar A)$, with $\bar A$ the frozen training median.
Political power saturates in economic weight: the first tenth of renewable capacity creates a
constituency where none existed; the eighth tenth adds little politically new. Its purpose is
to bound extrapolation, since REMIND takes the drivers far beyond their training range.

### Adjustment speed ($\lambda$)
The fraction of the distance to political equilibrium closed per year, estimated from history.
It lives **on the logit scale**, not on the 0–10 index. Only the electricity sector's speed
beats a persistence benchmark out of sample; the others are descriptive rates or inertia
floors, and those labels must travel with the numbers.

### Attractor ($S^{\text{eq}}$) versus ceiling ($S^{*}$)
Policy moves toward the ECM **equilibrium**, not toward the frontier. $\lambda$ was validated
against $S^{\text{eq}}$; using the frontier as the operational target imports the
$\gamma$-boundary degeneracy into the IAM. The frontier is a **bound and an exhibit**.

### CAPMF
The OECD Climate Actions and Policies Measurement Framework: the 0–10 policy-stringency index
that is the model's outcome variable. Its coverage — 48 of 248 countries — is the origin of the
coverage rule.

## The coupling

### Feasibility share ($\varphi$)
The fraction of *incremental* mitigation effort a region can realize:
$P^{*} = P^{\text{ref}} + \varphi(P^{\circ} - P^{\text{ref}})$. It discounts the **extra**
effort an ambitious pathway demands, never the policy a region already has. A parameter
refreshed between iterations — never a decision variable.

### Coupling severity ($\theta$)
The dial setting how hard $\varphi$ bites. **Declared and swept, never estimated** — no
historical experiment ever assigned carbon prices by political gap. $\theta = 0$ is the
uncoupled null and must reproduce the standard run exactly.

### Feasibility envelope
The division of labour: PFM supplies a bound and a speed limit; the IAM optimises *within* it.
Politics can only slow a pathway, never accelerate one.

### Bind mode
Which claim a coupled run makes: **ratio** (politics changes *where* abatement happens),
**level** (politics caps *how much* a region can do), **mild progression** (the price is
generated by political momentum, with no budget at all).

### Coverage rule / band rule
How countries CAPMF never observed receive a relative gap: **donor** (blend of nearest covered
countries in the model's own metric), **low band** (25th percentile, for genuine extrapolation
below support), or **median** (unmatched for structural reasons, not weakness). Each country
keeps its **own** ceiling and inherits only the gap. Replaces the old $\varphi = 1$ fallback,
which rewarded absence of data with maximal assumed capability.

### Anchor / reference / bound
$P^{\circ}$ the unconstrained cost-optimal price; $P^{\text{ref}}$ the current-policy price;
the **bound** what the coupling permits. The global **anchor** $A_t$ is REMIND's own price
level that the budget iteration keeps moving.

## Infrastructure

### Run-Group
A named directory under the Results Root holding every curated artifact of one sweep /
selection / post-processing run, plus its manifest. Several coexist; a report or a coupled run
is always rendered against exactly one. **The current group is `v5`** (deployed spec
`X-2079 WGIge|noRoL|VerAcc bothIncAP lev ctl:GDPq.Pop.Hyd fe:OECDp`). A number without its
Run-Group is not checkable.

### Fit Cache
The content-addressed model store: fits keyed by a hash of specification plus training data,
**shared across Run-Groups** — which is what makes a new group over an unchanged panel cheap.
An internal reuse mechanism, distinct from the Results Root, which is the curated
producer→consumer contract.

### Results Root
The directory the compute layer writes curated artifacts to and the report layer reads from.
Organised into Run-Groups.

### Run Record
The `manifest.json` inside a Run-Group: the authoritative record of what ran — versions,
training panel, steps, status, timings, cluster job id.

### Scenario registry
The list of REMIND pathways in `config.yml` that the one shared fitted model is projected onto.
Each entry names its gdx **and that gdx's own native region resolution**, which is a property
of the gdx, not a target.

### Gating scenario
The single scenario whose projection drives **model selection** — the high-ambition pathway,
because it is the stress test where explosive extrapolation surfaces. Selection runs once
against it; the selected model is projected onto every registered scenario.

### Shared specification
One specification, applied to both sectors, rather than a per-sector best. The cost of sharing
is measured and reported (each sector's own best, and what it gives up) — never silently
absorbed.

### ΔR²(theory) and theory fraction
**Not** interchangeable. ΔR²(theory) is the *incremental* explanatory power of the institutional
channels over region FE + controls + trend — the primary selection criterion. Theory fraction is
a *descriptive* decomposition of where the predicted level comes from, and region intercepts
dominate it.

### Maximin tournament and sanity walk
The selection rule: specifications passing the tier and VIF gates are ranked **maximin** —
worse-sector tier first, then worse-sector ΔR²(theory), then parsimony and fragility tie-breaks.
The **sanity walk** then descends that ranking and deploys the first specification with no severe
flag on its projections: scenario-blind (`minScenarioDelta`), extrapolation-dominated
(`supportShareGate`, 0.275), ceiling collapse (`ceilingFallGate`, 0.90) or γ at the boundary
(`gammaGate`, 0.999). `forced = TRUE` means the walk ran out of candidates. Rank 1 on this rule is
not dominance on any single criterion.

### Selection bootstrap
Resampling **whole countries** and re-running the entire selection, to quantify how much of the
chosen specification is an artifact of the sample. It is the evidence for what may be claimed
about channels — and against claiming a uniquely identified model.

### Driver support guard
The winsorization applied when scenario drivers leave the range that identifies the
coefficients, with `driverOutOfSupport` (clamped share) and `driverOutOfSample` (outside the
historical range) reported alongside every projected number. A projection with a high share is
being driven by the guard, not by the politics.
